Cookie & Local Storage Policy

ClearCue respects your privacy and avoids invasive third-party ad tracking or advertising cookies.

1. Do We Use Tracking Cookies?

No. ClearCue does not use third-party behavioral advertising cookies, data broker pixels, or cross-site tracking trackers.

2. Browser Local Storage Utilization

We utilize HTML5 localStorage strictly for functional and educational continuity purposes:

  • clearcue_auth_token: Stores your encrypted session JWT so you remain logged in across browser refreshes.
  • clearcue_backend_api_url: Allows configuring a custom Render backend connection if requested.
  • clearcue_user_progress_v2: Caches your practice history, flashcard mastery count, and streaks locally for fast offline access.

3. Clearing Your Data

You can clear your local data at any time by clicking Log Out or clearing your browser's site data in Developer Tools → Application → Local Storage.
